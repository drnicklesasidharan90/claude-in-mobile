About the model

Steady-state cardiac output is found where the RV function curve CO = P·(1−e−(RAP+2)/3) crosses the venous-return line VR = (MSFP − RAP)/RVR. The plateau P rises with contractility and heart rate over a limited range and falls with afterload. The crossing gives right-atrial pressure and forward output. RAP/CVP are pressures; JVP is an examination estimate of that pressure, not an interchangeable unit.

The equations and all threshold mappings are hand-tuned for concept demonstration. They are not a validated cardiovascular model and must not be used to infer a patient’s hemodynamics or treatment.
