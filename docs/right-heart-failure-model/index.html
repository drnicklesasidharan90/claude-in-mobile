🟨 Interactive Model of Right Heart Failure

Adjust the right ventricle’s loading conditions and contractility to see how forward output and systemic venous congestion change. The engine couples a Frank–Starling cardiac-function curve with a Guyton venous-return curve and solves for the operating point, then maps it to bedside signs.

About the model

Steady-state cardiac output is found where the RV function curve CO = P·(1−e−(RAP+2)/3) crosses the venous-return line VR = (MSFP − RAP)/RVR. The plateau P rises with contractility and heart rate and falls steeply with afterload (RV-specific). The crossing gives right-atrial pressure (≈ CVP ≈ JVP) and forward output, which drive the congestion signs. Numbers are illustrative, not for clinical use.
